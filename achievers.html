National Level Innovation • 2026

Autonomous Mobile Robot

Represented Team at National Level Innovation 2026

The EMBEDX robotics project team engineered a custom differential-drive autonomous rover with ultrasonic & IR sensor arrays, achieving sub-50ms reactive obstacle avoidance before national juries.

Team: EMBEDX Robotics Division • Department: EEE
Key Highlights:
  • Sub-50ms reaction latency on unmapped test terrain
  • Closed-loop PWM motor control with optical encoder feedback

Innovation Showcase • 2026

Smart Dustbin for Waste Segregation

Presented in Niral Thiruvizha 2026

Presented an automated touchless waste segregation prototype with ultrasonic depth fill telemetry and multi-compartment servo diversion at the prestigious Niral Thiruvizha 2026.

Team: EMBEDX Smart Systems Wing • Department: EEE
Key Highlights:
  • 100% contactless sanitary lid operation
  • Real-time bin volume telemetry preventing municipal overflow
