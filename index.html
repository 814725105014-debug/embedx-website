Engineering Beyond the Classroom

EMBEDX is the technical club of the Department of Electrical and Electronics Engineering at SRM TRP Engineering College, focused on Embedded Systems, Automation, IoT, Robotics, Control Systems, Electronics, and emerging smart technologies.

Learn

Deep hands-on technical learning covering microcontrollers, register architectures, embedded programming, and industrial protocols.

Build

Architect real hardware prototypes, autonomous rovers, smart sensor nodes, and industrial automation control systems from scratch.

Compete

Represent the institution in prestigious national-level hackathons, circuit debugging contests, and technical symposia.

Innovate

Turn bold conceptual engineering ideas into scalable, real-world solutions that create genuine technological and social impact.

About Our Community

Fostering the Next Generation of Embedded & Automation Engineers

EMBEDX is the official technical club of the Department of EEE at SRM TRP Engineering College, focusing on Embedded Systems, Automation, IoT, and Smart Technologies. The club aims to build technical skills, inspire innovation, and prepare students for real-world engineering challenges.

We operate as an agile, student-driven engineering powerhouse supported by departmental faculty mentorship, state-of-the-art laboratory infrastructure, and an unwavering commitment to hands-on craftsmanship.
