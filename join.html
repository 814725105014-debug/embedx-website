BECOME PART OF EMBEDX

Learn. Build. Experiment. Compete. Innovate.

Eligibility

Who Can Join EMBEDX?

Membership is open to enthusiastic engineering students across all years and departments interested in:

  • Embedded Systems & Firmware
  • Electronics & Circuit Prototyping
  • C / C++ / Arduino / Python Programming
  • IoT, Edge Computing & Cloud Telemetry
  • Robotics, Mechatronics & Motion Control
  • PLC & Industrial Automation
  • Control Systems & Sensor Integration
